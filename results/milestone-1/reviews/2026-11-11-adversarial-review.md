## First pass, on the plan and the declaration

VERDICT: SOUND-WITH-FIXES

1. [PLAN-BLOCKING] **The constants predate the data; the proposed decision rule does not.** Evidence: PREREG-v0.5.md:132 applies 0.70 to tracks on full-length calibration lives; :663 applies 75 to track calibration; specs/controls/naive_rag.md:45–55 makes its flags disclosures, never Ring 0 gates. Applying these to five-pair kinds and declaring survivors “usable for ranking” is new. Fix: call them exploratory screening flags and survivors “candidates for fresh validation”; disclose the new application and prior exposure to H. Low O establishes poor direct-evidence performance under this setup, not that the reader alone is defective.

2. [PLAN-BLOCKING] **The oracle-filter sequence conflates diagnosis, selection and repair.** Evidence: declaration §6 proposes repairing templates and deciding retention from the existing O repeats; PREREG-v0.5.md:313 requires each item to pass 2/3 evidence-only trials, and :326 freezes common IDs once. Old-template trials cannot validate repaired items. Fix: publish Milestone 1 on the unchanged, unfiltered inventory; report its prospective filter yield separately. New template versions need their own three oracle trials and a frozen retained inventory before subsequent system evaluation.

3. [IMPORTANT] **“Ring 1,” four rows and twelve lives are not interchangeable promises.** Evidence: RING0.md:420 requires committed fresh seeds, the lite audit and the eligible reference roster; :494 alternates G1/G2. Twelve total lives therefore provide only six action-scored G1 lives today: best two-sided p = .03125, not < .001. Fix: either meet Ring 1’s complete contract or call this a fresh-seed exploratory follow-up. Size by contributing G1 lives and anticipated effect, not merely an attainable p-value; twelve contributing lives permit .000488 but do not guarantee useful power.

4. [IMPORTANT] **The mixed table is publishable; several readings overstate it.** Evidence: RING0.md:29–34 gives the scripted reader gold-first option ordering. Computed R has competing values on all 20 value items yet scores 20/20. That is an evidence-availability diagnostic with privileged disambiguation, not observed live retrieval performance. Fix: distinguish the scripted columns visually; replace readings 1, 2 and 6 with observations about this scripted policy. “Wake is not measuring memory yet” should become “Wake’s low scores remain uninterpretable without its live oracle.” A strong O would implicate H’s complete presentation path, not uniquely its storage policy.

5. [IMPORTANT] **Presence checks do not establish valid control inputs.** Evidence: declaration §2 checks `in_context`; RING0.md:25–27 defines presence through token occurrence, while naive_rag.md:39–43 specifies chunk-level evidence matching. Matching booleans can conceal extra text, missing provenance, altered ordering or truncation. Fix: verify the actual delivered R/O payload against the pinned chunks/events, including order and boundaries; verify memory-off state and absence of answer-key/option-order material. Keep the placement fixed, but describe H-versus-controls as a system-path comparison: R and O share placement; H need not.

6. [IMPORTANT] **BLOCKED handling can change the experiment after outcomes arrive.** Evidence: declaration §2.4 requires scoring “in every arm,” potentially including optional systems and all repeats. A failed optional arm could erase the mandatory comparison; changing intersections also changes repeated-row means. Fix: define the mandatory four-row comparison set, repeat eligibility and incomplete-status rule now; show assigned and excluded counts. Compare optional rows separately. System failures remain scored failures, not exclusions (PREREG-v0.5.md:212–217). Identity-mismatched rows must remain separate, not enter pooled flags.

7. [IMPORTANT] **Whole-arm ordering leaves H unreplicated and time confounded.** Evidence: F,R,O,H,O,R,F,O gives H one middle slot and O three; identical served-model names do not demonstrate identical service behaviour. Fix: counterbalance arm order within life blocks, retaining clean resets and the declared repeat counts. Prefer an H repeat over optional systems if resources permit. This improves the experiment without importing the full board’s operating process.

8. [IMPORTANT] **Small-sample flag handling is still underdefined.** Evidence: with one repeat and no penalties, O needs 4/5 or 8/10 correct pairs to reach 75; at F=0,O=100, R needs 4/5 or 7/10 to reach 70%. A +1→−1 change moves 40 or 20 points, not one ordinary success increment. With three O repeats, the 75 threshold requires net sums ≥12/15 or ≥23/30. Fix: report signed-score margins and distinguish omissions from harmful errors; define how unequal repeats determine instability, and make unstable flags trigger no definitive ranking/repair classification. Suppress “near-solved” interpretations when O≤F or O≤0; retain the arithmetic only as disclosed diagnostics.

9. [IMPORTANT] **The proposed later calibration omits three frozen anchors.** Evidence: PREREG-v0.5.md:663 requires the **best of naive RAG, BM25, grep and recent window** to score ≤35, not naive RAG alone. Fix: keep Milestone 1’s four rows, but restore the complete anchor set in the later calibration track. Otherwise the benchmark can manufacture apparent memory difficulty by defeating one weak retriever.

10. [IMPORTANT] **Two historical statements need correction.** Evidence: live2/card.json records 20/20 floor units cached; live1 records 0/20. The floor was measured live once and reused, not independently rerun twice. H passed 18/40 actions in live1 and 17/40 in live2. Fix: publish those distinctions. The observed overall paired life-level p is **0.5**; .0625 is the theoretical minimum, not this result’s p.

11. [MINOR] **The pooled pair mean is legitimate descriptive arithmetic, but not the frozen headline.** Evidence: declaration §3 weights Drift’s 15 pairs three times Wake’s five; PREREG-v0.5.md:142–145 averages within lives and then equally across tracks. Fix: label it “pooled scored-pair diagnostic,” retain kind/per-life results, and do not carry its weighting into a pilot claim. Unequal exclusions must not silently reweight lives.

12. [MINOR] **Document the live-control extension and preserve the governance boundary.** Evidence: naive_rag.md:3 and :65 describe cached controls and exclude a Hermes-mode shim; PREREG-v0.5.md:435 describes T2 against cached controls. Fix: add a scoped Ring 0 extension/erratum for live controls, placement and repeats. This need not change a frozen scoring rule. Formal changes to scoring, calibration or bars require the corresponding freeze. Different-family adversarial review is valid interim scrutiny under PREREG-v0.5.md:58, but is not an eligible, calibrated SVP quorum.
